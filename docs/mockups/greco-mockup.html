Got it: your team spends a lot of time on the phone and you'd like some of that handled automatically. Three quick questions so I can point you in the right direction:

  1. Roughly how many calls do you get per day?
  2. What are most calls about?

About 120 a day, mostly booking and rescheduling. We use a cloud practice-management system.
Greco AI

With mostly booking calls, there are two common approaches:

  • An AI phone receptionist: answers calls, books or moves appointments in your system, and passes anything unusual to your staff.
  • Online booking with reminders: fewer calls in the first place, because patients book and reschedule themselves.

Many clinics combine both. I've drafted your brief on the right. Check it, then show matches. Please don't share patient details here; they aren't needed.
